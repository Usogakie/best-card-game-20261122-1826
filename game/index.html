You and Mira, Tidecaller, start at 20 health. Each turn you draw a card and your mana refills — spend it to summon creatures or cast spells. Creatures attack once a turn: into her creatures, or straight at her. A creature whose element beats its target's deals +1 damage. Reduce Mira to 0 health to win. Click a card to play it, click your creature then a target to attack. Space ends your turn, Esc cancels.
