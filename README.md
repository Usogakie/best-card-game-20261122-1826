Rune Clash — an original duel card game. You and Mira, Tidecaller, start at 20
health and battle with creatures and spells across three elements — Ember, Tide
and Thorn — each beating one and losing to another. Play cards, attack her
creatures or go straight for her, and reduce her to 0 health to win.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a card to play it,
click one of your creatures then a target to attack. Space ends your turn,
Esc cancels a selection.
